Today's day-ahead price

The price swings by hours, not by season: the same day holds both the expensive evening peak and the cheap midday hours when wind and solar flood the grid. Those dips are the whole opportunity a dynamic tariff exists to exploit.

What smart timing is worth

Shifting one 11 kWh EV charge into the cheapest hours is worth more than switching tariff type. The flat and dynamic-with-dumb-charging bars sit close together; the gap that matters opens only when the charge moves.
